# Rumbo AU — visual system

## Creative thesis

**Cartografía editorial de una espera compartida.** A contemporary travel
ledger built from dates, route lines and paper-like surfaces. It must feel calm,
useful and distinctly Chile-to-Australia without becoming a tourist postcard.

## Directions considered

1. **Cartographic ledger — selected.** Highest task fit, distinctive chronology,
   responsive resilience and low performance cost.
2. **Travel postcard — rejected.** Scenic imagery competes with data and leans
   on familiar Australia clichés.
3. **Operations dashboard — rejected.** Efficient but anonymous; it could belong
   to any SaaS product.

## Specificity locks

- **Composition:** wide chronological ledger with a date rail; detail pages are
  timeline-first.
- **Typography:** Archivo for interface hierarchy, DM Mono only for dates,
  attempts and operational metadata.
- **Palette:** paper `#F2EBDD`, ink `#14323B`, eucalyptus `#28685B`,
  terracotta `#C65C3C`, sun `#E0B642`, signal red `#A83E36`.
- **Imagery:** abstract Pacific route, cut-paper documents and topographic
  contours; no people or landmarks presented as evidence.
- **Motion:** a 180–240 ms route-line reveal and status stamp; removed for
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Proof:** 375, 768 and 1440 px captures; keyboard, zoom, overflow, contrast,
  empty/error/success and console checks.

Comparable captures are stored in `qa/` for the public board and application
detail at each target width. They are regenerated by `e2e/visual.spec.ts`.

## Image series bible

- Domain: community application tracking between Chile and Australia.
- Viewpoint: top-down editorial paper collage.
- Light: warm, diffuse, no photorealistic shadows.
- Material: torn paper edges, screen-print grain, thin cartographic ink.
- Palette: only the product tokens plus off-white variations.
- Negative space: preserve the left third for live HTML copy.
- Exclusions: people, flags as decoration, aircraft, kangaroos, koalas,
  government seals, logos, UI screenshots, accidental text or watermarks.
- Variation: route curvature and paper layers may vary; palette, grain and
  viewpoint may not.

## Asset provenance

Code-authored SVG assets are original to this repository. The editorial raster
hero is generated for this project with the prompt recorded alongside the final
file in `public/visuals/README.md`. Decorative assets use empty alt text;
informative illustrations receive factual Spanish alt text.
